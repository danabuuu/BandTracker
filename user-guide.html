Your friendly guide to managing repertoire, gigs, and setlists.

Getting Started

When you first open the app, you'll see a sign-in screen. You have two options:

Sign In

Enter your email and password to unlock full editing — add songs, build setlists, import data, and more.

Read-Only Mode

Browse the repertoire and gigs without signing in. Perfect for performers on stage who just need to view setlists.

Once signed in, use the Lock button in the top-right corner to sign out and switch back to read-only mode.

Repertoire Tab

This is your band's master song library — every song you know lives here.

Viewing Songs

Toggle between two display styles using the Cards / Spreadsheet button in the top-right of the tab:

  • Cards view — Visual song cards with all fields at a glance.
  • Spreadsheet view — A compact table, great for scanning a large library.

Searching & Sorting

  • Use the Search bar to filter by title, artist, genre, composer, or key — all at once.
  • Use the Sort dropdown to order by Title, Artist, Genre, Key, or Date Added.

Adding a Song signed in

  1. Click + Add Song to open the Add Song form.
  2. Fill in the fields — Song Title is required. Optional fields include: Artist, Genre, Composers, Voicing, Key, Duration, and Notes.
  3. Click Save to add the song.
💡 Tip: The Genre and Composers fields have smart autocomplete — start typing and suggestions from your existing songs will appear. Use ↑ ↓ to navigate and Enter to select.

Editing a Song signed in

Click the edit (pencil) icon on any song card or row to make changes. Save when done.

Deleting Songs signed in

  • Select one or more songs using their checkboxes, then click Delete Selected.
  • You'll be asked to confirm before anything is permanently removed.

Importing Songs via CSV signed in

  1. Click Import CSV.
  2. Select a .csv, .xlsx, or .xls file from your computer.
  3. The app matches columns automatically (case-insensitive).

Supported column names:

Exporting Your Repertoire

Click Export CSV to download your full song library. Great for backups or sharing with bandmates.

Gigs & Setlists Tab

Keep track of every gig — past and future — and build the perfect setlist for each one.

Viewing Gigs

Each gig is shown as a card with its name, venue, date, and time. Click a gig to expand it and see its setlist; click again to collapse it.

Use the Expand All / Collapse All buttons to quickly show or hide all gig details.

Searching & Sorting Gigs

  • Use the Search bar to filter by gig name, venue, or notes.
  • Use the Sort dropdown to order by Date, Name, or Venue.

Adding a Gig signed in

  1. Click + Add Gig.
  2. Fill in: Gig Name (required), Venue, Date, Time, and Notes.
  3. Click Save to create the gig.

Building a Setlist signed in

  1. Expand a gig card, then use the search box inside it to find songs from your repertoire.
  2. Click a result to add it to the setlist.
  3. Reorder songs by dragging and dropping, or using the ↑ ↓ arrow buttons.
  4. Click the ✕ next to a song to remove it.

Sharing a Setlist

Each gig has a Copy Link button that generates a shareable URL. Anyone with the link can open the app and jump directly to that gig's setlist — no login required.

Scroll Strips & Even Reality G2 Glasses

Scroll strips are specially prepared images of your sheet music designed to be displayed on Even Reality G2 smart glasses during a live performance. Instead of flipping pages, the music scrolls smoothly across the lenses at the correct tempo — so performers can keep their eyes on the conductor and their bandmates.

What is a Strip?

A strip is a single long image that stitches together every system of music for one voice part of one song. When the song plays on the glasses, the strip scrolls left automatically, advancing one screen-width per measure group. You create one strip per voice part (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, or Bass) for each song.

How to Create a Strip

  1. Open a song in the Repertoire tab and click Add Strip next to the voice part you want to create.
  2. Upload the PDF of the sheet music for that part — the app analyses the score automatically.
  3. On the selection page, click each system of music that belongs in the strip. Use the nudge arrows or ↑ ↓ keys to fine-tune the position of each selected system.
  4. Adjust the vertical padding (amber dashed lines) so the staff sits comfortably within the glasses display.
  5. Use the crop handles to trim any unwanted margins from either side.
  6. Set the BPM and number of bars in the sidebar so the scroll speed matches the song.
  7. Click Build & Add Part to save this voice part and immediately start the next one, or Build & Finish to return to the library.
💡 Tip: Build the Soprano strip first, then Alto, Tenor, and Bass in order. The part label dropdown auto-advances to the next unused part after each successful build.

Connecting the Glasses

The Even Reality G2 companion app on your phone connects to the Glasses Playback app running on your local network. To connect:

  1. Make sure the playback server (Vite dev server) is running on the same Wi-Fi network as the glasses.
  2. Open the Even Reality app on your phone and navigate to the App Hub.
  3. Scan the QR code below (or visit the GitHub link) to find the Glasses Playback app repository and set it up on your local network.
  4. On the glasses, use the temple button to select your voice part, choose a setlist, and start performing.

Tips & Tricks

  • Read-only mode is perfect for performers on stage — no accidental edits possible.
  • The shareable setlist link is handy for sending the night's setlist to bandmates before a gig.
  • The app syncs automatically with the cloud, so all band members see the same data in real time.
  • Genre and Composers autocomplete keeps your data consistent across the whole library.
